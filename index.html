01 · Project overview

Built for rescue, survey and field operations.

The objective is one serviceable platform that can fly to a location, travel on the water surface and move across suitable ground without changing vehicles. The proposal focuses on Bihar and Indian use cases such as flood response, river survey and agricultural monitoring.

✦

Air · Flight mode

Four corner-mounted 15-inch propellers on carbon-fiber tube arms are intended for scouting, mapping and carrying a compact sensing payload.

  • 2207 1500KV motor concept
  • Pixhawk 6C + GPS / IMU
  • Target endurance: 25 minutes — unverified
≈

Water · Surface mode

A buoyant speedboat-style bow hull provides surface transport. Two green CALANDIS CDT-9024 ducted thrusters are proposed at the rear sides.

  • Low-drag rear thruster placement
  • Waterproof connectors and sealing
  • Target speed: 8 knots — unverified
⬡

Land · Rover mode

Four folding wheel modules are intended to deploy under the hull for movement on suitable firm or moderately uneven terrain.

  • 80 mm NBR wheels
  • 120 mm folding struts
  • Positive lock / feedback required
Important system-level feasibility check

The proposal states 4.2 kg empty mass plus 1.5 kg payload (about 5.7 kg before any excluded items) and 6 kg total aerial thrust. That is approximately a 1.05:1 thrust-to-weight ratio from the stated figures, leaving almost no lift margin. Recalculate all-up mass and measure thrust using the final motor, propeller, ESC and battery combination before attempting flight.

03 · System architecture

Six major modules. One supervised platform.

The architecture separates propulsion hardware, control electronics and mission software. Each powered mode should be mutually interlocked so incompatible actuators cannot run at the same time.

PART 01

Hydrodynamic hull

V-bow concept; split shell; gasket groove; arm, thruster and wheel hardpoints. Proposed carbon-fiber shell with 6061-T6 brackets.

Envelope target: 1200 × 800 × 250 mm · shell 2 mm proposal · hull mass target 1.2 kg (verify)
PART 02

Aerial system ×4

350 × 25 mm carbon tube arms, clamp mounts, wire channels, motor mounts and 15-inch three-blade props.

2207 / 1500KV motor concept · ESC rating to be calculated
PART 03

Water propulsion ×2

CALANDIS CDT-9024 green ducted thruster reference. Rear-side mounts, sealed cable entries and guarded impeller geometry.

User-specified: 12–24 V · 3.5 kgf each · OD 90 × 120 mm · verify vendor data
PART 04

Retractable wheels ×4

80 mm NBR wheels, folding struts, pivot hardware and a positive mechanical lock with position feedback.

120 mm strut proposal · 20 kg-cm servo concept · load / torque testing required
PART 05

Battery & electronics bay

Central low-mounted battery to aid balance, with EPDM gasket, sealed connector, strain relief and serviceable upper cover.

6S 16 Ah / 22.2 V nominal · box 200 × 150 × 80 mm · verify XT60 current rating
PART 06

Camera & sensing pod

Under-nose 3-axis gimbal with isolated mount. GPS, IMU, barometer, LiDAR and sonar provide navigation and environment inputs.

4K target · yaw 360° concept · gimbal weather protection must be verified

08 · Electronics & autonomy

Control stack and mission software.

Separate flight-critical stabilization from higher-level perception and wheel control. Loss of companion-computer software should not remove the flight controller’s independent stabilization or failsafe capability.

⌘

Flight & navigation

ArduPilot-based configuration is proposed with Pixhawk 6C, GPS, IMU, barometer and standard flight failsafes. Tri-mode switching needs custom implementation and bench / hardware-in-loop tests.

◉

Perception & robotics

Raspberry Pi 5 runs Python computer vision for object detection and mission logic. ROS 2 is proposed for wheel control and higher-level module coordination.

⌁

Ground station & data

Mission Planner / QGroundControl with 2.4 GHz RC, 5.8 GHz video, 900 MHz telemetry and optional 4G LTE according to permitted spectrum and deployment conditions.

Failsafe philosophy

Do not implement an unconditional automatic switch to another domain. Air-to-water, water-to-land and land-to-air transitions have different physical risks. Each transition needs environmental checks, actuator-lock feedback, operator override and a validated safe response.

From a prototype to field trials.

Initial markets are hypotheses for customer discovery. Demonstrate measurable performance and obtain operational approvals before promising mission outcomes.

Capture imagery and map surface conditions. Water-quality sampling and depth mapping need separate sensors, calibration and protocols.

⌁

Agriculture

Inspect field boundaries, irrigation ponds and crop conditions with an appropriate camera or sensing payload.

⌖

Assess hard-to-reach terrain and shorelines. Public-safety or border use needs proper authorization, privacy safeguards and rules compliance.

Potential customer groups

NDMA / SDRF, state agriculture departments, universities and labs, environmental NGOs, river survey teams and mapping startups. Approach each with a controlled demonstration and a clear operating envelope.

Market hypothesis

The proposal estimates a ₹120 crore Indian amphibious-drone market by 2028. Treat this as an unverified internal estimate until supported by a documented market study and defined market boundary.

Baseline issue to resolve first

The stated 4.2 kg airframe mass, 1.5 kg payload and 6 kg total thrust do not show a useful flight reserve. Verify whether the mass includes battery, landing gear, thrusters, wiring and gimbal, then reselect propulsion and update the mass budget.

Potential phases: solar-assisted charging, multi-vehicle coordination and AI-assisted flood mapping. A 5 m diving mode is a major redesign involving pressure hull, buoyancy control, seals, propulsion and sensing—not a software-only upgrade.

12 · Intellectual property workspace

Patent Design — preliminary disclosure concept.

This section structures the concept for discussion with a patent professional. It is not a legal opinion, patentability conclusion, validated claim set or evidence that a filing has been made.

PATENT DESIGN COLUMN

Integrated amphibious mobility and propulsion platform

Investigate the specific mechanical coordination of a buoyant hull, aerial arms, rear ducted water propulsion and positively locked retractable wheel modules under a mode-supervised controller.

Working title

“An Integrated Tri-Domain Unmanned Vehicle with Convertible Aerial Propulsion, Water Thrusters and Retractable Ground Mobility”

Draft abstract

The proposed unmanned platform comprises a buoyant hull, aerial propulsion modules, rear-mounted ducted water propulsion units, retractable ground-wheel assemblies, a sealed central electronics compartment and a controller that supervises domain-specific actuator states. Mechanical locks, sensor feedback and mode-transition interlocks are intended to reduce the risk of incompatible actuators being energized during conversion between flight, surface-water and ground operation.

Draft language only. Confirm novelty and adequacy of description before any filing.

Candidate inventive aspects to investigate

01 · Folding gear package

Wheel strut geometry, hinge / lock design, water clearance and landing loads in stowed and deployed states.

02 · Rear thruster integration

Thruster mounting, nozzle protection, seal / service interface and packaging relative to hull buoyancy.

03 · Mode interlock strategy

Specific sensor-confirmed mechanical and electrical sequence to prevent conflicting air, water and land states.

04 · Serviceable modular architecture

Defined modular interfaces for hull, arms, battery bay, gimbal and replaceable propulsion modules.
